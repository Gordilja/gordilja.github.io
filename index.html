Delivery Idle

A multiplayer game demo made in Unity featuring local player data storage (name, rating, coins, address). It utilizes a Master/Client connection where clients connect via a mobile app (Android, iOS) to place food orders. The player can accept or decline orders, receiving ratings and gold based on their performance.
